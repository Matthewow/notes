### materially change things

**Meaning:** 对结果、情况或决定产生重大影响。

**Examples**

- Removing the export limit would materially change things for enterprise customers that need to share large reports.
- The proposed database migration will not materially change things for users, but it should reduce maintenance work for the team.

**Note:** *Material* can mean “材料,” but as an adjective it can also mean “important” or “substantial.” *Materially* means “significantly” here, not “in a way related to physical materials.”

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-07-23-more-tangible -->
### more tangible

**Meaning:** 更具体、更容易理解、感受到或看到。

**Examples**

- The prototype makes the proposed workflow more tangible for the team than a written specification alone.
- After we mapped the customer journey, the impact of the delayed launch became more tangible to the leadership group.

**Note:** It is often used for ideas, plans, benefits, or risks that become clearer or more real.

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-07-23-be-willing-to-join-the-effort -->
### be willing to join the effort

**Meaning:** 愿意参与并帮助完成一项需要共同投入的工作。

**Examples**

- I am willing to join the effort, but I need a clear owner and timeline before I can commit time.
- Several engineers were willing to join the effort to improve the onboarding flow before the next release.

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-07-23-punctuation -->
### punctuation

**Meaning:** 标点符号；也指正确使用标点的规则。

**Examples**

- Clear punctuation makes long technical instructions much easier to read during an incident.
- Please check the punctuation in the release notes before we publish them to customers.
