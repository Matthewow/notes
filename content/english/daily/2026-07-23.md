<!-- english-item-id: 2026-07-23-everything-is-up -->
### everything is up

**Meaning:** 所有系统、服务或功能都在正常运行并且可用。

**Examples**

- After the deployment, everything is up: the API is responding, the dashboard loads, and users can complete checkout.
- In this morning's stand-up, Maya confirmed that everything is up and there are no production incidents to report.

**Note:** In engineering status updates, *up* means operational, not increased.

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-07-23-product-call -->
### product call

**Meaning:** 产品层面的决策；主要根据用户需求、产品策略和业务取舍来决定的事。

**Examples**

- I think this needs to be a product call, because both implementation options are technically feasible but serve different user needs.
- Engineering can explain the trade-offs, but choosing whether to simplify the onboarding flow is ultimately a product call.

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-07-23-scope-creep -->
### scope creep

**Meaning:** 范围蔓延；项目需求在没有正式控制或批准的情况下逐渐增加。

**Examples**

- We need to reduce scope creep before the launch, because several small requests have added new screens and delayed the original plan.
- Adding one more user type may sound minor, but it can become scope creep if we do not update the timeline and priorities.

**Note:** Here, *creep* means to grow slowly and gradually; it does not mean *creepy* or scary.

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-07-23-blast-radius -->
### blast radius

**Meaning:** 影响范围；某项改动、故障或决定可能波及的系统、用户、团队或业务流程。

**Examples**

- Before changing the payment API, we should map its blast radius, including checkout, refunds, mobile clients, support tools, and legal disclosures.
- The database incident had a larger blast radius than expected because several internal services depended on the same connection pool.

**Note:** The phrase comes from the physical area affected by an explosion and is used metaphorically in software and product work.

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-07-23-a-turn-off -->
### a turn-off

**Meaning:** 让人失去兴趣、好感或继续使用意愿的事。

**Examples**

- Long and confusing legal disclosures can be a turn-off for users who only want to understand the payment terms before checking out.
- For many candidates, a company that never replies after an interview is a major turn-off.

**Note:** As a noun, write it as *a turn-off*.

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-07-23-firstborn -->
### firstborn

**Meaning:** 第一个孩子；在幽默说法中，指极其珍贵、代价很高的东西。

**Examples**

- We are not taking anyone's firstborn; we only need the team to spend ten minutes filling out the short feedback form.
- The approval process feels so complicated that it seems like they are asking for my firstborn just to create a test account.

**Note:** *Take someone's firstborn* is a humorous exaggeration for demanding an unreasonable sacrifice, not a literal request.

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-07-23-staggered -->
### staggered

**Meaning:** 错开安排的；分批或在不同时间开始、进行或结束的。

**Examples**

- The previous program was not staggered, so every participant reached the end-of-day deadline at the same time and overloaded the support team.
- We staggered the rollout across three regions so that we could monitor the first group before enabling the feature for everyone.

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-07-23-double-down-on -->
### double down on

**Meaning:** 加倍投入；更坚定地继续做某件已经认为重要或有效的事。

**Examples**

- We should double down on asking individual team members to add their perspective to the talk, rather than relying on one presenter.
- Since the prototype tested well with users, the team decided to double down on that approach in the next sprint.

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-07-23-surface-by-surface -->
### surface by surface

**Meaning:** 按界面或接触点逐个处理、检查或设计。

**Examples**

- During the accessibility review, we checked the product surface by surface, from the mobile checkout page to the internal support dashboard.
- The migration cannot be verified with one test; we need to test each user-facing surface by surface.

**Note:** Before a noun, it is often hyphenated: *a surface-by-surface review*.

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-07-23-lopsided -->
### lopsided

**Meaning:** 不平衡的；一边明显更多、更强、更重或更有优势。

**Examples**

- The planning discussion felt lopsided because product and engineering were represented, but customer support had no one in the meeting.
- The workload is lopsided this sprint: one developer owns most of the urgent bug fixes while the rest of the team has few assigned tasks.
