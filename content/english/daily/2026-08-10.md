### wrap up early

**Meaning:** 提前结束会议、工作或某项任务。

**Examples**

- If there are no other topics to discuss, I’m happy to wrap up early and give everyone some time back.
- We finished the release review faster than expected, so we wrapped up early.
- Let’s try to wrap this up early, but keep each other informed about any blockers as we approach the code-complete date.
