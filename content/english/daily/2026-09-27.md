### pre-emptively

**Meaning:** 提前采取行动，以防止预期的问题发生。

**Examples**

- We increased the timeout pre-emptively because we expected the migration to make some requests slower.

**Note:** *Pre-emptively* emphasizes preventing a problem; *proactively* more broadly means taking initiative.

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-09-27-a-broad-brush-overview -->
### a broad-brush overview

**Meaning:** 只介绍主要内容、不涉及细节的概览。

**Examples**

- I’ll give you a broad-brush overview of the architecture so you can understand how the main services fit together.

**Note:** *Broad-brush* and *high-level* have similar meanings here; using both is usually unnecessary.

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-09-27-cumbersome -->
### cumbersome

**Meaning:** 使用或处理起来繁琐、不方便的。

**Examples**

- The approval process is cumbersome because every small change requires three separate reviews.
- This approach can become too cumbersome to be worth the effort as the number of customers grows.

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-09-27-blue-green-deployment -->
### blue-green deployment

**Meaning:** 蓝绿部署；在两套环境中运行不同版本，验证新版本后将流量切换过去。

**Examples**

- We’ll use a blue-green deployment so we can validate the new version in a separate environment before switching production traffic to it.

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-09-27-mandate -->
### mandate

**Meaning:** 正式要求；由组织或有权作决定的人提出的指令。

**Examples**

- The company’s mandate is to move all production services to the new authentication system before the end of the year.

**Note:** A *mandate* emphasizes authority; a *non-negotiable requirement* emphasizes that a requirement cannot be changed or relaxed.

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-09-27-nuts -->
### nuts

**Meaning:** 太离谱；很不合理。

**Examples**

- Asking the team to take on another project when they’re already covering two extra responsibilities is kind of nuts.

**Note:** This is informal and expresses strong criticism. *Unreasonable* is more neutral.
