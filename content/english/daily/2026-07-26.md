<!-- english-item-id: 2026-07-26-dive-straight-in -->
### dive straight in

**Meaning:** 不作过多铺垫，直接开始讨论或处理某事。

**Examples**

- We only have thirty minutes, so I’ll dive straight into the launch risks and leave questions until the end.

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-07-26-a-natural-extension-of -->
### a natural extension of

**Meaning:** 某件事的自然延伸；与现有工作、关系或产品顺理成章地衔接。

**Examples**

- Supporting Afterpay felt like a natural extension of our existing partnership with the company.


<!-- english-item-id: 2026-07-26-blow-past-a-target -->
### blow past a target

**Meaning:** 大幅超过一个目标或预期数字。

**Examples**

- The forecast is conservative, so the team hopes the product will blow past its annual bookings target.

**Note:** This is informal and confident. In a formal report, use *significantly exceed the target*.


<!-- english-item-id: 2026-07-26-absorb-the-cost -->
### absorb the cost

**Meaning:** 自己承担某项费用，而不把它转嫁给其他人。

**Examples**

- Lyft would absorb the higher processing cost instead of charging additional interest to riders.

**Note:** The transcript’s *take up the interest burden* is understandable but unnatural here. *Absorb the cost* or *bear the cost* is more natural.

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-07-26-call-out -->
### call out

**Meaning:** 特别指出或强调某个重要信息、风险或例外情况。

**Examples**

- I want to call out one limitation: gift-card purchases will not be supported at launch.

**Note:** In workplace communication, *call out* often means “highlight.” In other contexts, it can mean publicly criticizing someone.

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-07-26-work-through -->
### work through

**Meaning:** 通过讨论或逐步处理来解决问题、分歧或复杂细节。

**Examples**

- We still need to work through the legal restrictions before gift-card support can be added.

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-07-26-the-game-plan -->
### the game plan

**Meaning:** 为实现目标而制定的总体行动方案。

**Examples**

- The game plan is to launch the core payment flow first and negotiate support for gift cards in phase two.

**Note:** This is common but slightly informal workplace language.

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-07-26-know-what-youre-getting-yourself-into -->
### know what you’re getting yourself into

**Meaning:** 清楚自己将要面对的责任、风险或复杂情况。

**Examples**

- The interface should clearly explain the repayment schedule so users know what they’re getting themselves into.

**Note:** This often suggests that the decision may have risks, costs, or difficult consequences.

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-07-26-surface-an-option -->
### surface an option

**Meaning:** 在界面或流程中的适当位置向用户显示一个选项。

**Examples**

- If the original card is declined, the app can surface Afterpay as an alternative payment option.

**Note:** *Surface* is commonly used as a verb in product and software discussions.


<!-- english-item-id: 2026-07-26-fall-into-a-bucket -->
### fall into a bucket

**Meaning:** 根据某些特征或条件被归入某个类别。

**Examples**

- Only a small percentage of riders are expected to fall into the bucket where the final charge exceeds the authorization limit.

**Note:** *Bucket* is informal product and analytics language for a category or group.

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-07-26-take-a-calculated-risk -->
### take a calculated risk

**Meaning:** 在分析可能性和后果后，有意识地承担一个可控风险。

**Examples**

- The team decided to take a calculated risk and handle rare payment shortfalls through the existing debt-recovery flow.


<!-- english-item-id: 2026-07-26-nail-down -->
### nail down

**Meaning:** 把尚未明确的计划、规则或细节最终确定下来。

**Examples**

- The marketing team still needs to nail down the exact conditions that will trigger the airport-ride promotion.

**Note:** This is common informal workplace language. *Finalize* is a more formal alternative.

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-07-26-come-into-the-picture -->
### come into the picture

**Meaning:** 开始与某个情况相关，或开始在其中发挥作用。

**Examples**

- Membership purchases and Lyft Cash top-ups come into the picture when the transaction is above the minimum amount.


<!-- english-item-id: 2026-07-26-shift-a-mental-model -->
### shift a mental model

**Meaning:** 改变用户理解某个产品、流程或概念的方式。

**Examples**

- Making Afterpay the default method could shift the user’s mental model from using it occasionally to using it for recurring purchases.

**Note:** A *mental model* is a person’s internal understanding of how something works.

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-07-26-take-something-into-account -->
### take something into account

**Meaning:** 在分析情况或做决定时考虑某个因素。

**Examples**

- We need to take the higher processing cost into account before encouraging broader use of Afterpay.

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-07-26-a-stretch -->
### a stretch

**Meaning:** 不太合理、不太现实，或超出当前可接受范围的想法。

**Examples**

- Making an expensive payment method the default for every ride may be a stretch.

**Note:** In this usage, *a stretch* means the proposal goes beyond what seems reasonable or practical.
