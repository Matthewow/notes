<!-- english-item-id: 2026-08-24-take-a-hit -->
### take a hit

**Meaning:** 受到负面影响；出现下降、损失或打击。

**Examples**

- Our current metrics will probably take a hit while users adjust to the new onboarding flow.
- Performance may take a hit if we enable detailed logging for every production request.

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-08-24-delegate-implementation -->
### delegate implementation

**Meaning:** 委托实现；由单独的类或对象负责处理特定职责的实现方式。

**Examples**

- Having a separate delegate implementation is fine as long as its responsibilities are clearly defined.
- We moved the callback logic into a delegate implementation so the main component could remain focused on rendering.

**Note:** *Delegate* is often a specific software design concept. *Delegated implementation* has a broader meaning: an implementation assigned to another component.

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-08-24-provisioned -->
### provisioned

**Meaning:** 已创建、分配并配置好，可以使用的。

**Examples**

- I’m trying to transfer the account ID that has already been provisioned for the customer.
- The infrastructure team provisioned a new test environment before the integration work began.

**Note:** An account, environment, device, or other resource is commonly *provisioned*. An ID is more often *assigned*, unless the ID represents the provisioned resource.

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-08-24-be-at-parity-with -->
### be at parity with

**Meaning:** 与另一个产品、平台或功能达到同等水平。

**Examples**

- The cloud-code CLI experience is now at parity with the web-based development experience.
- We need the Android workflow to be at parity with iOS before we launch the feature publicly.

<!-- english-item-id: 2026-08-24-trickle-in -->
### trickle in

**Meaning:** 人们陆续、零散地到达或加入。

**Examples**

- We can get started while people continue to trickle into the meeting.
- Questions trickled in throughout the afternoon after we shared the proposal with the wider team.
